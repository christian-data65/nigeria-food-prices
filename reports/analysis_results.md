# Nigeria Food Prices Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes food prices across different states in Nigeria to understand price differences, identify expensive and affordable food items, and examine changes in food prices over time.

## Analysis Results

### November 2024

1. **Highest Food Prices:** Frozen chicken had the highest average price at ₦6,130.51, followed by boneless beef at ₦6,001.32.

2. **Lowest Food Prices:** Agric eggs (medium size) had the lowest average price at ₦247.26.

3. **Biggest Monthly Price Increases:** Onion bulb recorded the largest increase of ₦626.48 compared with October 2024. Vegetable oil followed with ₦427.88.

4. **Biggest Monthly Price Decreases:** Brown beans recorded the largest decrease of ₦77.54, while white beans decreased by ₦56.62.

## Key Findings

- Frozen chicken was the most expensive item among the five highest-priced food items in November 2024.
- Agric eggs had the lowest average price among the five lowest-priced food items.
- Onion bulb recorded the biggest monthly price increase.
- Brown beans and white beans were the only two items among the five lowest monthly changes that experienced price decreases.
