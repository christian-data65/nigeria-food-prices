# Nigeria Selected Food Price Watch — Business Questions

These are the 10 questions used for the analysis of the Nigeria Selected Food Price Watch data.

1. What are the five highest-priced food items in the latest reporting month?
2. What are the five lowest-priced food items in the latest reporting month?
3. Which food items recorded the biggest month-to-month price increases?
4. Which food items recorded the biggest month-to-month price decreases?
5. Which geopolitical zone has the highest average food prices?
6. Which geopolitical zone has the lowest average food prices?
7. Which food item has the biggest price difference across the six geopolitical zones?
8. Which food item has the smallest price difference across the six geopolitical zones?
9. Which food items recorded the highest percentage price increases across the reporting periods?
10. Which food items had the most stable prices across the reporting periods?
