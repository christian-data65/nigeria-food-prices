# PROJECT BRIEF
Nigeria Food Prices Analysis

## 1.Project Overview

This project analyzes food prices across different states in Nigeria to understand price differences, identify expensive and affordable food items, and examine changes in food prices over time.

### 2. Stakeholders and Their Needs

Stakeholder	Information Needed
CEO	An overview of food price trends across Nigeria.
Procurement Team	States with the lowest food prices for selected food items.
Sales Team	Food items and states with the highest prices and significant price increases.

### 3. Business Questions

These are the 10 questions used for the analysis of the Nigeria Selected Food Price Watch data.

What are the five highest-priced food items in the latest reporting month?
What are the five lowest-priced food items in the latest reporting month?
Which food items recorded the biggest month-to-month price increases?
Which food items recorded the biggest month-to-month price decreases?
Which geopolitical zone has the highest average food prices?
Which geopolitical zone has the lowest average food prices?
Which food item has the biggest price difference across the six geopolitical zones?
Which food item has the smallest price difference across the six geopolitical zones?
Which food items recorded the highest percentage price increases across the reporting periods?
Which food items had the most stable prices across the reporting periods?

### 4. Data Sources

1. **NBS Food Price Watch:** Monthly average prices of selected food items across Nigerian states.

The dataset will be inspected and analyzed to understand food price trends and differences across Nigerian states.
